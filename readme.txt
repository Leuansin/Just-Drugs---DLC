# 💊 JustDrugsDLC — The Ultimate Drug Mod for Project Zomboid  
### ✅ Compatible with Build 41 & Build 42

---

> This mod is designed to be the **most complete and realistic Drug Mod** for Project Zomboid.  
> It’s so detailed that you might even mistake it for an *official DLC*.

---

## 🧠 Overview

Have you ever wanted **realistic drugs** in your Project Zomboid world — with **balanced loot tables, detailed effects, withdrawal, abstinence, and addiction systems**?

**Search no more.**  
This mod adds a complete and immersive experience with dozens of new items, mechanics, and visual/audio effects.

---

## 🧩 Features

- 🧬 **3 New Traits**
- 💊 **31+ Unique Drugs**
- 📚 **10+ Books** to learn drug crafting
- 💉 **3 New Medicines** to counter overdoses & poisonings
- ⚗️ **Drug Crafting System**
- 🚑 **Improved Overdose System**  
  Find **Narcan** to survive fatal fentanyl or other drug overdoses
- 🔄 **Enhanced Addiction System**  
  Recover from addiction or fall into it through repeated use
- 😖 **Improved Withdrawal System**
- 👁️ **NEW: Hallucination System** (Visual + Audio)
- 📦 **Pack & Unpack System**  
  Create packages from **5g → 10g → 20g → 100g → 1kg**
